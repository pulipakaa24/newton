Add `SolverXPBD(joint_coloring=True)` to solve joints Gauss-Seidel by color (no two joints of a color share a body) instead of summing all joint corrections per body, and `joint_extra_iterations` for joint-only passes after the main iterations. On a 44-body humanoid at 4 iterations / 1.25 ms, coloring with relaxation 0.8 reduces the joint-anchor drift from 14.8 mm to 3.4 mm (median).
